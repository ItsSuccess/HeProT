ABOUT HeProT

Bridge the gap
from healthcare to tech.

Changing fields can leave you unsure what to learn, how to apply your healthcare experience, or how to stay accountable. HeProT is a community to help you make that transition through purposeful learning, collaboration and practical work.

OUR COMMUNITY

HeProT is not a training group or course provider. Members take responsibility for their own learning and career development; the community offers accountability, collaboration and shared support.

WHO IT’S FOR

All healthcare professionals and healthcare students exploring tech.

OUR APPROACH

The HeProT BUILD Framework

A simple path from real-world problems to practical solutions.

B

Begin with Problems

Identify meaningful real-world problems across healthcare, businesses, communities, industries and society.

U

Understand the Challenge

Investigate the problem, its underlying causes, affected populations and existing gaps.

I

Invest in Relevant Skills

Identify and develop the technical and professional capabilities required to address the identified problems.

L

Launch Solutions

Collaborate, experiment and apply acquired capabilities to develop practical solutions, products and prototypes.

D

Demonstrate Impact

Test, evaluate, improve and showcase what has been built, creating tangible evidence of competence and impact.

FAQ

A few quick
answers.

Do I need technology experience?+

No. Curiosity, consistency and a willingness to contribute matter more than prior experience.

Do I need to leave healthcare?+

No. Technology can complement your current work or lead to new paths.

How do I apply to join HeProT?+

Complete the application form. Your application will be reviewed; submitting it does not automatically mean your membership is approved. You can continue to the WhatsApp Community after submitting.

Does HeProT guarantee a job or income?+

No. HeProT makes no guarantees about jobs, income, scholarships or funding.
